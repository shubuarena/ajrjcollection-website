What we do

More than money: we help build the business.

An idea needs more than funding to succeed. We work alongside founders from the first conversation to launch and beyond.

1

Invest

We provide early funding to get promising ideas off the ground, so founders can focus on building rather than chasing money.

2

Build

We help shape the product, brand and plan: from naming and design to testing with real customers and getting ready to launch.

3

Grow

After launch we stay involved, helping each business find its audience, learn from feedback and grow steadily over time.

How we work

From idea to launch, one step at a time.

We keep things simple and practical, and we test early so good ideas get stronger and weaker ones are improved quickly.

  1. ListenWe start with the idea, the problem it solves and the people it is for.
  2. TestWe try a simple version with real people before spending big.
  3. BuildWe turn what works into a proper product and brand.
  4. LaunchWe bring it to market and keep improving it together.

What we look for

Ideas that make everyday life better.

We are open to ideas in many areas. These are the things that matter most to us.

  • A real need. A clear problem that people actually have.
  • Something different. A fresh approach, not a copy of what already exists.
  • Room to grow. A business that can reach many people over time.
  • Done responsibly. Honest, safe and respectful of customers' privacy.
  • Committed people. Founders who care deeply about what they are building.
  • Built to last. Long-term value, not a quick win.
